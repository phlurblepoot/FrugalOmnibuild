# Blank documents

`new.docx`, `new.xlsx` and `new.pdf` (a blank PDF form) are the en-US blank
files from ONLYOFFICE's document-templates repository, unchanged:

- Source: https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/document-templates (folder `new/en-US`)
- Commit: 0a52fb76fa53fddf7d70da28878537cde3d29421 (2026-09-18)
- Copyright: Ascensio System SIA
- License: Apache License 2.0, included here as `LICENSE`

The app copies one of them when someone makes a new blank document
(`POST /api/documents/new`, ONLYOFFICE Phase 3).
